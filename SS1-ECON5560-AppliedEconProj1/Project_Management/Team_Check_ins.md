# Team Check-In — Five Questions


*Use these for a short team check-in (~15 min, no instructor). Talk through each one honestly. The point isn't to sound fine — it's to catch small problems now, while they're easy to fix, instead of at week 8.*


**1. Are we actually following our charter?** Pull up your charter. Are we doing what we agreed on — communication, meeting cadence, response times? Where have we drifted, and does the charter need updating?


**2. Is the work landing on the right people — and is everyone carrying a fair share?** Is each person doing work that fits what they're good at, or is someone stuck on the thing they're worst at? Is anyone doing too much or too little?


**3. How are we communicating and coordinating — really?** Are we talking enough to stay in sync, or are people working in silos and finding out too late that pieces don't fit? Is anyone hard to reach?


**4. Who's watching the whole project, and are we on track?** Is someone keeping an eye on the big picture and the deadlines, or is everyone heads-down on their own part? Are we actually where we need to be for the Week 7 draft?


**5. Is anything going unsaid?** Is there a frustration, a worry, or a problem nobody's named yet? Say it now, directly and kindly — this is the moment for it.
