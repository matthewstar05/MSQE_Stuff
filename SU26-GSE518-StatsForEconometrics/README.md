## Course Overview

GSE 518 provides the rigorous mathematical and statistical foundation required for graduate econometrics. Topics cover probability theory, univariate and multivariate random variables, moment-generating functions, properties of random samples, classical point estimation (Method of Moments and Maximum Likelihood Estimation), finite-sample evaluation criteria, and asymptotic theory.

## Curriculum & Topics

1. **Notes 1 — Set Theory**: Sample spaces, events, set operations, De Morgan's laws, partitions.
2. **Notes 2 — Probability Theory**: Axioms of probability, counting methods, combinations and permutations.
3. **Notes 3 — Conditional Probability & Independence**: Law of Total Probability, Bayes' Theorem, statistical independence.
4. **Notes 4 — Random Variables**: Discrete and continuous random variables, probability mass/density functions.
5. **Notes 5 — Distribution Functions**: Cumulative distribution functions (CDFs) and their properties.
6. **Notes 6 — Transformations of Random Variables**: Monotone transformations, Jacobian method, probability integral transform.
7. **Notes 7 — Expectations, Moments & MGFs**: Expected values, variance, higher moments, moment generating functions and uniqueness.
8. **Notes 8 — Commonly Used Distributions**: Bernoulli, Binomial, Poisson, Uniform, Normal, Gamma, Chi-square, Beta, Exponential.
9. **Notes 9 — Multiple Random Variables**: Joint, marginal, and conditional distributions, covariance, correlation, bivariate normal.
10. **Notes 10 — Properties of a Random Sample**: i.i.d. random variables, sample mean and sample variance properties, distribution of sample statistics under normality.
11. **Notes 11 — Point Estimation & Method of Moments (MM)**: Parameter spaces, analogy principle, sample vs. population moments.
12. **Notes 12 — Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE)**: Likelihood function, score equations, Hessian, information matrix, invariance principle.
13. **Notes 13 — Methods to Evaluate Estimators**: Mean squared error (MSE), unbiasedness, efficiency, Cramér-Rao Lower Bound (CRLB), consistency.
14. **Notes 14 — Asymptotics**: Convergence in probability (WLLN), convergence in distribution (CLT), Continuous Mapping Theorem, Slutsky's Theorem, delta method, asymptotic normality of MLE.
